Pad Thai

Description

Pad Thai is a popular Thai stir-fried noodle dish made with thin rice noodles, eggs, tofu, and a choice of protein such as chicken, shrimp, or prawns. It is tossed in a flavorful sauce made from tamarind, fish sauce, palm sugar, and lime juice, creating a distinctive balance of sweet, sour, and savory flavors.

Often garnished with crunchy roasted peanuts, fresh bean sprouts, chopped chives, and a wedge of lime, Pad Thai offers a delightful combination of textures and aromas. This iconic Thai street food is typically served hot and delivers a satisfying blend of tanginess, subtle sweetness, and rich umami flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 (12 ounce) package rice noodles
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 pound skinless, boneless chicken breast halves, cut into chunks
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
  • ⅛ tablespoon crushed red pepper
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • ¼ cup crushed peanuts
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Soak rice noodles in cold water until soft, 30 to 50 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  3. Meanwhile, heat butter in a wok; add chicken and sauté until browned. Remove chicken and set aside.
  4. Heat oil in the wok over medium-high heat. Crack eggs into hot oil and cook until firm. Stir in chicken and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add softened noodles, sugar, fish sauce, vinegar, and red pepper; mix well until noodles are tender. Adjust seasonings to taste.
  6. Stir bean sprouts into wok and cook for 3 minutes.
  7. Serve topped with green onions, crushed peanuts, and a wedge of lemon.
